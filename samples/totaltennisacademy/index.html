Questions

Good to know

I don't know my level. Where do I start?

Create an account and one of our pros will rate you beginner, intermediate or advanced. A private lesson is a great way to get rated quickly. Once you're rated, you'll see the group classes at your level.

What if I can't make a class?

Cancel online any time before it starts and the class credit goes back to your account, so you can use it on another day.

How do I pay?

Group classes are paid online by card, a month at a time. Private lessons are paid directly to your pro by Zelle.

Do you teach kids?

Yes. We run junior programs, camps and leagues, as well as adult programs. Call or email us to find the right fit.

Do you string racquets?

Yes. Ask us about our stringing services.
