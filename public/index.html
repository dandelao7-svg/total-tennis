Questions

Frequently asked

Do my students need to download an app?

No. TennisDesk runs in any phone or computer browser. You share one link and students sign up in about a minute.

Where does the money from lesson sales go?

Straight into your own Stripe account. TennisDesk never receives or holds your students' payments. You can also record cash, check and Zelle payments.

How long does setup take?

About 15 minutes to add your courts, pros and prices. Connecting Stripe takes a few minutes more if you already have an account.

Can I use my own words, like "court rental" instead of "private lesson"?

Yes. Pick the wording that fits your academy in Setup and it changes everywhere students and pros see it.

What happens when I cancel?

Cancel any time from the Billing page. You keep access to the end of the month you've paid for, and you can ask us for a copy of your data.

Is my academy's information safe?

Passwords are scrambled, payment keys are encrypted, and everything is backed up daily. Your student list is yours. We never sell it.
